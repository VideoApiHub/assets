# WealthMinute01 - Image Generation Prompts & Animation Guide

## Script Breakdown & Visual Concepts

### Scene 1: "Have you ever noticed how some people seem to effortlessly glide through life while the rest of us are grinding away just to keep our heads above water?"
**Duration:** ~4 seconds

**Image Prompt:**
"Cartoon illustration split-screen style: Left side shows a person effortlessly floating/gliding through clouds with money symbols gently floating around them, happy expression, relaxed posture. Right side shows a person drowning/struggling in water filled with coins and bills, stressed expression, arms reaching upward. Bright, contrasting colors: blue water with golden coins on left side, golden light around floating figure. Modern flat design style, vibrant colors."

**Objects to Include:**
- Golden coins & bills (floating on left, sinking on right)
- Clouds (supporting left figure)
- Water waves (containing right figure)
- Currency symbols ($, €, ¥)

**Animation Guide:**
- Left figure: gentle floating motion, slight upward bobbing
- Right figure: struggling/sinking motion, splashing effect
- Coins: fall from top to bottom, some orbit the figures
- Background: gentle cloud drift

---

### Scene 2: "They are not necessarily smarter than you. They did not inherit secret family fortunes and they are definitely not using some mystical money manifestation technique they learned from a guru on Instagram."
**Duration:** ~5 seconds

**Image Prompt:**
"Cartoon illustration showing three side-by-side figures of equal height and intelligence symbols above each (lightbulbs, brain icons). Center figure has a wealthy elder passing money in glowing envelope (crossed out with red X). Right figure has a phone showing Instagram influencer/guru with mystical aura (also crossed out with red X). Background gradient from corporate office to spiritual/mystical imagery. Clean, educational infographic style with check marks and X marks indicating myths vs reality."

**Objects to Include:**
- Lightbulbs (equal above all figures)
- Brain symbols
- Glowing envelope (with X)
- Instagram phone (with X)
- Checkmarks and X marks
- Crystal ball/mystical elements (faded/crossed out)

**Animation Guide:**
- X marks: appear with quick swipe/slash animation
- Lightbulbs: subtle glow pulse
- Figures: slight head shake animation
- Crossed-out elements: fade in and quickly X over

---

### Scene 3: "Yet somehow they are building wealth while you are wondering where your paycheck disappeared to again."
**Duration:** ~4 seconds

**Image Prompt:**
"Cartoon illustration showing two parallel timelines: Left timeline shows wealthy person with stacked coins/bars growing like a chart going upward, surrounded by growing plants and compound interest symbols. Right timeline shows regular person with empty wallet, paycheck arriving and immediately disappearing (bills labeled: rent, food, taxes, healthcare, etc.). Use contrasting warm colors (left) and cool colors (right). Include visual metaphor of water flowing downhill on right side, flowing uphill on left. Modern, clean design."

**Objects to Include:**
- Stacked coins (left, growing)
- Upward arrow graph (left)
- Growing plant/tree (left, wealth growth metaphor)
- Empty wallet (right)
- Bills/expenses (rent, food, taxes, healthcare, etc. - right)
- Paycheck (appearing and disappearing - right)
- Compound interest symbols (left, spiraling upward)

**Animation Guide:**
- Left side: coins stack upward, growth arrow climbs, plant grows taller
- Right side: paycheck enters and splits into bills flying outward
- Chart line: draws from bottom-left to top-right (left timeline)
- Bills: cascade down and away on right side
- Both timelines: move left to right

---

### Scene 4: "Here is what is really happening. The top one percent do not just have more money than everyone else. They think about money in a completely different way."
**Duration:** ~5 seconds

**Image Prompt:**
"Cartoon illustration showing a brain in the center split into two halves: Left half (90% label) shows scattered, reactive thoughts - bills, debt, immediate needs, money anxiety symbols. Right half (1% label) shows organized, strategic thinking - systems diagram, compound growth chart, investment symbols, long-term planning boards. Use pyramid structure showing 99% at bottom (scattered thinking) and 1% at top (strategic thinking). Color scheme: left side warm/chaotic, right side cool/organized. Include lightbulbs with different brightness levels. Educational infographic style."

**Objects to Include:**
- Brain visualization (split perspective)
- Percentage labels (1% vs 99%)
- System diagrams (right side)
- Growth charts and arrows
- Light bulbs (different brightness)
- Pyramid structure
- Money symbols arranged differently (chaos vs order)
- Planning boards/calendars (right side)

**Animation Guide:**
- Brain visualization: pulses with different intensity on each side
- Pyramid: builds from bottom to top
- Left side thoughts: swirl chaotically
- Right side thoughts: organize into structured patterns
- Light bulbs: brighten from left to right progression
- Charts: draw themselves with smooth animation

## Output Specifications for All Images

**Dimensions:** 1920x1080 pixels (1080p video format)
**Style:** Modern cartoon/flat design illustration
**Color Palette:** 
- Primary: Blues (#0066CC, #00CCFF), Golds (#FFD700, #FFA500)
- Secondary: Greens (#00CC66), Reds (#FF3333)
- Neutral: Whites, Light Grays for contrast

**Quality:** High quality, vector-style illustrations suitable for motion graphics

---

## Implementation Notes

Each image should have:
1. **Clear visual hierarchy** - focal points obvious
2. **Readable at small sizes** - animation-friendly design
3. **Layered backgrounds** - allows for parallax effects during motion
4. **Consistent character design** - same cartoon person across scenes when needed
5. **Neutral expression elements** - allow for animation overlay

## Animation Timing

- Scene 1: 0-4 sec (effortless vs grinding)
- Scene 2: 4-9 sec (debunking myths)
- Scene 3: 9-13 sec (wealth building vs expenses)
- Scene 4: 13-18 sec (mindset difference - final revelation)
